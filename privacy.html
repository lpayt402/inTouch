Privacy policy

Revised October 27, 2026

Here With Us keeps your projects and media in the app on your device. This page explains what happens when you use the app, choose to share something, contact support, use TestFlight or visit this website.

Your Here With Us library

Here With Us stores project titles, optional names and messages, pages, photos, source videos, voice recordings, camera recordings and finished movies in its local library. Media preparation and processing happen on your device. Ordinary local creation, recording, playback and export need no account. The app has no separate Here With Us account system, developer-hosted library, advertising, analytics, tracking or automatic media uploads. Optional private feeds use your existing Apple account and iCloud, as described below. The developer does not receive your local library through the app.

New projects, finished-video additions and editable copies need a title before final saving. Names, descriptions, dates and dedications are optional. The app does not request location, birthdate or school information. Contacts are optional and used only as described below. Use material you have the right to copy and share, and get consent before recording another person. Adults should assist children with creation and sharing.

Optional private feeds beta

Feeds are off until you explicitly choose Use Feeds or Open shared feed. Enabling Feeds checks your existing Apple account and uses Apple's CloudKit private and shared iCloud service. Local creation, recording, playback and export remain available without enabling Feeds.

In supported beta builds, you may deliberately choose a photo, a video, a GIF or an ordered set of photos for a post. Availability depends on the build, feed format and device. The app prepares a separate copy on the device; your original remains in its source. Selected photos are prepared as upright JPEG copies within the app’s published limits. A supported GIF is prepared as a finite, one-cycle video: transparent areas appear black and very short delays are adjusted. The prepared copy may differ from the original and does not preserve every source detail or metadata.

Before publishing, review the prepared media, required title, optional caption, chosen feed and audience. Publishing uploads the selected prepared copy and post metadata to the chosen feed. It does not automatically upload your Library, original source files, recordings or editable projects.

Each feed has an owner and a private audience. Invited people can read the owner’s shared media; the feed’s retained earlier and future posts may be visible to accepted participants. On a supported comment-enabled feed, an admitted person may write their own comments and likes in a separately authorized text lane. They cannot edit the owner’s media or manage feed membership by virtue of commenting. These text lanes are created under the feed owner’s private iCloud storage; they do not use a commenter’s private iCloud quota. Comments and likes may be unavailable on older feeds or app versions. A separate feed and explicit invitation are needed to publish back.

Check the people and the named feed before confirming an access change. Checking an address does not send an invitation; sharing a private link is a separate action. A pending invitation or a completed sharing sheet does not prove delivery or acceptance. Per-share changes may be pending or uncertain; wait for the app’s confirmation rather than assuming all access has changed.

Pausing Feeds on this device does not revoke invitations, end existing shares or delete published posts. Where the app offers Stop sharing it affects the named feed; for some comment-enabled formats, stopping all access at once is unavailable and people must be removed individually. Leave feed affects your access to one invited feed. Independent copies already saved or shared elsewhere cannot be recalled.

Permissions and selected files

Microphone access records video sound or your explanation when you choose to record. Camera access supports photo and video capture, page scanning and Camera + Voice narration. Voice Only narration does not need camera access. Photos import reads only the pictures or videos you select; Save to Photos requests permission only to add a finished video. You can change camera, microphone and Photos permissions in system Settings.

To help invite someone to a private feed, you may search contacts already allowed to the app or choose contacts through iOS. On iOS 18 and later, you may approve selected contacts for the app; that limited access remains until you change it in iOS Settings. A search of allowed contacts happens on the device and is limited to the entered name. Earlier supported iOS versions use the native contact picker. The app does not request full Contacts access as part of this flow or upload your address book. When you deliberately choose and check an email or phone, that selected address is sent to Apple to resolve the person; checking does not send an invitation.

Files import reads selected pictures, PDFs and movies through native system interfaces and copies them into app storage. A cloud provider may download a file you choose. Page and PDF preparation and media validation remain local; picker locations are not stored as permanent project assets.

Optional page adjustment uses on-device Apple Vision analysis for reading orientation and crop safety. Recognized text is discarded during processing; it is not saved, logged, transmitted or shown as extracted content. The app does not recognize page turns or analyze live camera text.

Copies you choose to share

Photos, Files, Share and AirPlay act only when you choose them. You choose the destination in native system interfaces. A page PDF includes the selected page pictures and anything private already visible in them. AirPlay streams the finished movie to the receiver you select. Copies outside the app and device backups may use cloud services according to your system settings; their providers and recipients control those copies.

Closing a share sheet does not confirm that a recipient received a copy. Watch a finished video before sharing it and check the destination yourself.

Retention, recovery and deletion

Projects and media remain in the local library until you delete them or delete the app. Deleting a project removes its owned pages, source videos, recordings and finished movies. Add Commentary and editable copies own independent media, so deleting either project does not remove the other's source. Removing a Show & Tell item keeps its original source video until that project is deleted.

Prepared Share and Files attachments may remain in app storage after a sheet closes or a project is deleted so another app can finish reading them. These prepared copies are excluded from backup. Bounded cleanup removes only recognizable abandoned preparation work that was never offered to another app. Promoted attachments are not automatically deleted because of age or sheet completion. Deleting the app removes its local library and prepared copies. Copies saved to Photos, Files, other apps, recipients or system backups must be managed separately.

Where supported, the owner can delete a published post or delete a feed through the app after confirmation. Deleting a feed removes its shared feed posts and verified conversation data. Originals in Photos, iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive, Library and Studio stay, as do independent saved copies. Other people’s separately owned media and already saved copies remain separate. Confirm the app’s result; an interrupted request may need checking.

Interrupted recordings may remain available for recovery. Keep the app installed while troubleshooting. Update it in place; do not delete it as an update step.

Private-feed drafts, prepared media, pending publication requests and replaceable downloads are stored separately from Library projects. Deleting a Library project does not remove an independent published feed copy. Retiring a local composer draft removes only that draft and its prepared media; submitted requests and published posts remain separate. Removing a download, pausing Feeds or deleting the app does not delete published posts from iCloud or end existing shares. Copies saved to Photos, Files, other apps, recipients or backups must be managed separately and cannot be recalled by Here With Us.

TestFlight beta testing

TestFlight is Apple's beta service. During beta testing, Apple collects crash logs, usage information and personal information and shares relevant diagnostics and feedback with the developer. Feedback and screenshots you submit are also shared and may identify you. This is separate from Here With Us's local library processing. Review screenshots and feedback for private material before sending them. Read Apple's TestFlight privacy information.

Support email

If you email support, the information in your message is sent through your mail service to the support inbox. Send only what you intend to share through that communication. Avoid sending children's private media or personal pages by default. Support requests are retained as long as necessary to address inquiries. To ask for deletion of a support message, email outreach@402infosec.com. Any request is handled subject to applicable obligations and available copies.

This website

This is a static site with no forms, analytics, trackers or embedded third-party assets. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub says it automatically logs website visit data, collects service-usage information including visitor IP addresses, and processes personal data for safety and security. GitHub's privacy statement describes retention as dependent on purpose and legal obligations; we have not verified a fixed retention period for GitHub Pages visitor IP logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

Policy changes and questions

Changes to this policy will be dated on this page. Questions about privacy or a support-message deletion request can be sent to outreach@402infosec.com.
