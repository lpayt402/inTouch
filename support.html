A little help along the way

Here With Us support

Make a story, one moment at a time.

Make your first video

  1. Start a project. Choose Read a Book or Show & Tell in Create. Select pages, photos or videos and review their order. Add the required title before saving; names and other details are optional.
  2. Record your story. Choose Voice Only or Camera + Voice. Record while tapping Next page or Next item, then choose Finish recording.
  3. Watch before sharing. Open Preview & adjust and choose Create video to make the finished movie. Watch it before choosing Share, Photos or Files.

Add a Finished Video begins with a selected movie; review it and add a title before Add video saves its own Library item. Add Commentary makes a separate Show & Tell project and preserves the saved original. Narrate a Slideshow and Create an Original Story are also available in Create.

Private feeds and people

Feeds are optional. In supported beta builds, choose a photo, video, GIF or ordered photos, then review the prepared copy, required title, named feed and audience before Publish. A supported GIF plays one finite cycle as video; transparency appears black and very short frame delays are adjusted. Your local Library is not uploaded automatically. Features may vary by build, feed format and device.

On a feed you own, open Manage feed to edit its name or display name, manage posts or people, and delete the feed where supported. Deleting a post or feed removes shared app copies; originals in Photos, iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive, Library and Studio stay. Independent saved copies remain. On some comment-enabled feeds, Stop sharing for everyone is unavailable; use Manage people to review each person’s removal and wait for each listed share to be confirmed.

To find a person, search a name or use the native contact picker. On iOS 18 or later, search uses contacts already allowed to the app on your device; iOS can let you approve additional contacts, which remain allowed until changed in Settings. Select one person and the address they use with iCloud. Checking the chosen address with Apple does not send an invitation. Review the named feed and access, confirm the change, then share a private invitation link only if you choose. A sharing sheet or pending status does not prove delivery or acceptance.

Some newer feeds support comments and likes after access is verified. A commenter can change their own text, not another person’s posts or membership. The owner’s private iCloud storage holds the feed’s comment lanes. Other people’s contributor-owned media remains in their separate source feeds. When an access result is uncertain, use the app’s check or retry guidance instead of assuming it completed.

If something goes wrong

  • Keep the app installed. Deleting it removes its local library. After an interruption, return to the project or Get Ready to check for a kept recording.
  • Allow time for the finished video. Finish recording keeps the take; Create video renders the movie. Appearance changes require Update video. Your last successfully saved movie remains available until a replacement succeeds.
  • Check permissions. Camera and microphone permissions are managed in Settings. Voice Only remains available without camera access. Importing photos uses only selected media; saving to Photos needs add permission.
  • Review pages before recording. Confident book-page orientation corrections are reversible. Ambiguous pages may need manual rotation. A complete spread counts as one page picture. After recording, make an editable copy to change locked pages.
  • Check source sound. Show & Tell playback is muted while recording to avoid microphone feedback. Off, Low or Full controls source sound in preview and the finished video.
  • Check shared copies separately. Closing a share sheet does not confirm delivery. Open the copy at its chosen destination. Prepared attachments may remain locally so receiving apps can finish reading them.

Limits and availability

  • Books and still-photo projects allow up to 50 pages. A complete spread counts as one page picture.
  • Show & Tell allows up to 30 visuals. Each source video may be up to 10 minutes, 512 MiB, 3,840 pixels per edge and 8.3 million pixels per frame. Retained source videos together may occupy up to 1 GiB per project, including movies whose visual was removed. Show & Tell takes stop at 15 minutes.
  • Each visit to a video starts from its beginning, plays once and holds its last frame until Next item. Revisiting starts a new visit. During recording, there is no trimming, looping, pausing or seeking. Saved movies have normal playback controls.
  • Finished output is landscape 16:9, with the complete artwork fitted inside it. Separate import byte and time limits also apply.

Native scanning, camera, microphone modes and AirPlay depend on the device and available system routes. Specific compatibility has not been verified on every device. Cutout is not a release feature.
