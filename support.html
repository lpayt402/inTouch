A little help along the way

inTouch support

Make a story, one moment at a time.

Make your first video

  1. Start a project. Choose Read a Book or Show & Tell in Create. Give it a title, then add pages, photos or videos in order. Names and other details are optional.
  2. Record your story. Choose Voice Only or Camera + Voice. Record while tapping Next page or Next item, then choose Finish recording.
  3. Watch before sharing. Open Preview & adjust and choose Save video to make the finished movie. Watch it before choosing Share, Photos or Files.

Add a Finished Video starts with a title and keeps the reviewed imported movie as its own Library item after Add video. Add Commentary makes a separate Show & Tell project and preserves the saved original. Narrate a Slideshow and Create an Original Story are also available in Create.

If something goes wrong

  • Keep the app installed. Deleting it removes its local library. After an interruption, return to the project or Get Ready to check for a kept recording.
  • Allow time for the finished video. Finish recording keeps the take; Save video renders the movie. Appearance changes require Update video. Your last successfully saved movie remains available until a replacement succeeds.
  • Check permissions. Camera and microphone permissions are managed in Settings. Voice Only remains available without camera access. Importing photos uses only selected media; saving to Photos needs add permission.
  • Review pages before recording. Confident book-page orientation corrections are reversible. Ambiguous pages may need manual rotation. A complete spread counts as one page picture. After recording, make an editable copy to change locked pages.
  • Check source sound. Show & Tell playback is muted while recording to avoid microphone feedback. Off, Low or Full controls source sound in preview and the finished video.
  • Check shared copies separately. Closing a share sheet does not confirm delivery. Open the copy at its chosen destination. Prepared attachments may remain locally so receiving apps can finish reading them.

Limits and availability

  • Books and still-photo projects allow up to 50 pages. A complete spread counts as one page picture.
  • Show & Tell allows up to 30 visuals. Each source video may be up to 10 minutes, 512 MiB, 3,840 pixels per edge and 8.3 million pixels per frame. Retained source videos together may occupy up to 1 GiB per project, including movies whose visual was removed. Show & Tell takes stop at 15 minutes.
  • Each visit to a video starts from its beginning, plays once and holds its last frame until Next item. Revisiting starts a new visit. A take cannot be trimmed, looped, paused or seeked within the app.
  • Finished output is landscape 16:9, with the complete artwork fitted inside it. Separate import byte and time limits also apply.

Native scanning, camera, microphone modes and AirPlay depend on the device and available system routes. Specific compatibility has not been verified on every device. Cutout is not a release feature.
